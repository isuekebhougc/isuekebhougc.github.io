Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

This policy covers Northframe Ads and UGC Engine, the internal tool we use to run our video-ad work. UGC Engine is used only by Northframe Ads, with our own Google account (isuekebhougc@gmail.com). It is not offered to the public.

Google data we access

  • Gmail: to send our own emails (approval requests, pitches, follow-ups, delivery notes) and to check whether a contact has replied, so we stop emailing them.
  • Google Sheets: to read and update our own project spreadsheet of leads, jobs and scripts.
  • Google Drive: to store the videos we approve and to share view-only links with the brand they were made for.

We use this data only to run our own business. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties, except the services that process it on our behalf (listed below). Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Information about brands we contact

We keep business contact details (brand name, product page, contact name and work email) and notes on our contact with them. We use public information from product pages to write ad scripts. Anyone can ask us to stop contacting them by replying "no thanks". We then mark them so they are never emailed again.

Services that process data for us

  • Google (Gmail, Sheets, Drive, and Gemini, which writes our ad scripts)
  • Paystack, which handles invoices and payments. We never see or store card details.
  • Our AI video provider, which receives the script and product image for a video.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep records only as long as we need them for our work and our accounts. To ask what we hold about you, or to have it deleted, email isuekebhougc@gmail.com.
